Citation
========

If ``speclib`` contributes to published work, cite both:

#. the archived Zenodo release corresponding to the version used,
   `doi:10.5281/zenodo.7868049 <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7868049>`_; and
#. Rackham & de Wit (2024), *Toward Robust Corrections for Stellar
   Contamination in JWST Exoplanet Transmission Spectra*,
   `doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ad5833
   <https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ad5833>`_.

This is the repository's preferred citation guidance as recorded in
``CITATION.cff``. The Zenodo concept DOI resolves to the release archive; cite
the exact version used where your reference manager supports version-specific
metadata.

Model library citations
-----------------------

Also cite the data product underlying your result:

* PHOENIX-ACES-AGSS-COND-2011: `Husser et al. (2013)
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201219058>`_.
* SPHINX I V4: `Zenodo data record 11392341
  <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11392341>`_ and `Iyer et al. (2023)
  <https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/acabc2>`_, as requested by the data
  record.
* PHOENIX/1D NewEra: `Hauschildt et al. (2025)
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202554171>`_ and the `V3.4 FDR release
  <https://doi.org/10.25592/uhhfdm.17935>`_.

See :doc:`models/index` for the exact product that each selector loads. The
repository's contribution-credit policy is available on :doc:`contributing`.
